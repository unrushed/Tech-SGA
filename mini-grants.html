Mini Grants

The Application Process

  1. Figure out the Details

    Whenever requesting a mini-grant, it's important to understand the total cost of your event and where you can find funding. Remember SGA only covers 1/3 of the total cost of the grant, so the other 2/3 must be funded elsewhere. Once the details are finalized, you can proceed with the proposal. You can find a sample proposal here.

  2. Submit Your Application

    When you finish your proposal, you can submit it through the online submission form

    Complete A Grant Application

  3. Check your Email

    After submitting your application, the treasurer will then review it for any issues. After the treasurer's review, you will be notified by the secretary that you will be required to do short presentation to the senate explaining the why you need the money and how it benefits the student body.

  4. Short Presentation

    A powerpoint presentation is not required, and discouraged. You will simply need to present how much money you need and how it will benefit the student body. After the presentation, the senate will have the opportunity to ask questions and finally vote on your request.

  5. Acquire Grant Funding

    Once your mini-grant has been approved, you will have access to the funds imediately. The funds will be allocted you.
